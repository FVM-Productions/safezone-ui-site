Privacy Policy

SafeZone UI is built so your work stays yours. Your videos, photos and projects never leave your device.

Effective: September 22, 2026

The short version. There is no account and no sign-in. Everything you import, record or export is processed and stored on your phone, and is never uploaded to us or to anyone else. The only data that leaves your device is what our advertising provider, Google AdMob, collects to show ads. That data is described in full below.

Who we are

SafeZone UI is published by Freddyville Media Productions LLC ("we", "us"). If you have any question about this policy, email safezoneui@fvmstudios.com.

What stays on your device

These are handled entirely on your phone. We cannot see them, and we never receive them:

  • Videos and photos you open. The app reads them from your library only when you choose them, to show them under the platform overlay.
  • Takes you record. The camera and microphone are used only while you are recording, and the result is saved on your device.
  • Exported frames. These are saved to your photo library only when you tap export or share.
  • Projects, thumbnails and settings such as your theme, overlay opacity and preferred platform.

Deleting a project removes its files, and deleting the app removes all of it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

SafeZone UI is free and paid for by ads, which are served by Google AdMob. To do that, Google's software in the app collects the following. None of it includes your photos, videos or projects.

Your choices. On iPhone, SafeZone UI asks your permission before your advertising identifier can be used for tracking. If you decline, you still get the full app, with ads that are not personalized. You can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings → Privacy → Ads.

Google handles this data under its own policies. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy.

Safe-zone updates

Platforms sometimes change their layouts. SafeZone UI may check Google Firebase for updated safe-zone measurements. The two Google services involved are Firebase Remote Config and Firebase Installations; the check sends an anonymous installation identifier and basic diagnostic information, and never includes your media. Without a connection, the app uses the measurements built into it.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not run accounts, profiles or analytics of our own.
  • We do not upload, view or keep your videos, photos or projects.

Children

SafeZone UI is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or the CCPA), you may have the right to access or delete personal data. We do not hold personal data about you, because what you create stays on your device. For advertising data, use the device settings above or contact Google through its privacy tools. We are happy to help with any request at safezoneui@fvmstudios.com.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date. If a change is significant, we will also note it in the app's release notes.

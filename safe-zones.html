How to use a template

Open the platform's page, download the PNG and place it on the top track of your editor at full frame. Line up your text and subject inside the green box, then hide the overlay before exporting.

Check your actual video, not just a template. SafeZone UI puts these zones over your own clip on your phone, so you can pinch and drag until nothing important is covered. It's free, with no account.

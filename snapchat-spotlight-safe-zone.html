Common questions

What is the Snapchat Spotlight safe zone?

It's the part of a 9:16 video that stays visible once Snapchat Spotlight draws its interface on top. On a 1080×1920 canvas, it's a 884×1320 px box starting 56 px from the left and 200 px from the top.

How much of a Snapchat Spotlight video gets covered?

About 44% of the frame sits under the creator name and caption along the bottom, the action column on the right, and the Spotlight header at the top. Roughly 56% stays clear.

Where should captions go on Snapchat Spotlight?

Keep burned-in captions above 1520 px from the top (at least 400 px off the bottom edge) and left of 940 px, so the right-hand buttons don't cover them.

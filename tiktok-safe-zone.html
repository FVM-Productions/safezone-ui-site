TikTok safe zone

On a 1080×1920 9:16 video, keep text, faces and logos inside a 870×1340 px box: 220 px down from the top, 360 px up from the bottom, 60 px in from the left and 150 px in from the right. Everything outside it can end up under the username, caption and sound credit along the bottom, the like, comment, save and share column on the right, and the Following / For You tabs at the top.

Common questions

What is the TikTok safe zone?

It's the part of a 9:16 video that stays visible once TikTok draws its interface on top. On a 1080×1920 canvas, it's a 870×1340 px box starting 60 px from the left and 220 px from the top.

How much of a TikTok video gets covered?

About 44% of the frame sits under the username, caption and sound credit along the bottom, the like, comment, save and share column on the right, and the Following / For You tabs at the top. Roughly 56% stays clear.

Where should captions go on TikTok?

Keep burned-in captions above 1560 px from the top (at least 360 px off the bottom edge) and left of 930 px, so the right-hand buttons don't cover them.

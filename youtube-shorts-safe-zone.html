Common questions

What is the YouTube Shorts safe zone?

It's the part of a 9:16 video that stays visible once YouTube Shorts draws its interface on top. On a 1080×1920 canvas, it's a 864×1420 px box starting 56 px from the left and 160 px from the top.

How much of a YouTube Shorts video gets covered?

About 41% of the frame sits under the channel name, subscribe button and title along the bottom, the like, dislike, comment, share and remix column on the right, and the search and menu icons at the top. Roughly 59% stays clear.

Where should captions go on YouTube Shorts?

Keep burned-in captions above 1580 px from the top (at least 340 px off the bottom edge) and left of 920 px, so the right-hand buttons don't cover them.
